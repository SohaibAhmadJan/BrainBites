# Version Snapshot: v2.8.6 (Mathematical Precision & Hub Mastery)

This document serves as the official record of **Version 2.8.6** of the BrainBites application. This version represents the absolute peak of visual fidelity for the discovery hubs, focusing on mathematical alignment and seamless background integration.

## Key Features in v2.8.6

### 1. High-Fidelity Discovery Cards
- **Seamless Surface Integration**: Replaced standard `Card` components with custom `Surface` containers for the Explore Hub. This ensures 100% color fill coverage, eliminating any visual gaps or "colourless" areas at the bottom of cards.
- **Mathematical Centering Engine**: Refactored the internal layout of Category Grid items to use root-level centering. Icons, titles, and fact counts are now perfectly balanced and centered, providing a professional and symmetrical aesthetic.
- **Unified Background Logic**: Gradients and solid bases are now applied directly to the surface foundation, ensuring they remain perfectly intact and gap-free even as cards expand vertically due to text scaling.

### 2. Hub Refinements
- **Balanced Featured Insights**: Centered the content in the Featured carousel for a more premium, "editorial" feel.
- **Polished Surprise Me**: Applied the same "Zero-Gap" and centering rules to the Magic Discovery card for a unified hub experience.

### 3. Versions Available
- **Version 1.0 - 2.8.4**: Foundation, functional milestones, and data portability.
