# Version Snapshot: v2.7.1 (Visual Discovery & Design Polish)

This document serves as the official record of **Version 2.7.1** of the BrainBites application. This version focuses on significant visual enhancements to the discovery system and final polish of the "Explore" hub.

## Key Features in v2.7.1

### 1. Differentiated Discovery Sections
- **Unique Purposes**: Clearly separated the discovery roles. The **Home Screen** focuses on general trending popularity, while the **Explore Hub** now provides "Featured Insights" curated for category diversity.
- **Diverse Selection**: Updated the Explore logic to ensure the featured carousel showcases facts from a wide range of different psychology topics simultaneously.

### 2. Vibrant Visual Polish
- **Dynamic Category Gradients**: Replaced the static card designs in the Explore hub with beautiful, linear gradients that automatically adapt to each fact's category color.
- **Enhanced Hierarchy**: Improved the visual weight of cards with bold category badges and adjusted typography for better readability against colorful backgrounds.

### 3. Motion & Interaction
- **Staggered Animations**: Restored and refined the entrance animations for both categories and featured insights. Items now flow onto the screen with a premium, cascading effect.
- **Responsive Feedback**: Maintained consistent ripple effects and elevation changes across all new discovery components.

### 4. Technical Improvements
- **Stable Versioning**: Formally released as v2.7.1 with updated splash screen labels.
- **Code Cleanliness**: Resolved remaining Modifier and import warnings across the Explore and Splash screen implementations.

## Versions Available
- **Version 1.0 - 2.5**: Foundational features and functional notifications.
- **Version 2.6**: Initial Explore Hub & Search.
- **Version 2.7.1**: (CURRENT) Visual Discovery & Design Polish.
