# Version Snapshot: v2.7.9 (In-Depth Fidelity & Mastery)

This document serves as the official record of **Version 2.7.9** of the BrainBites application. This version represents the culmination of several major feature iterations, focusing on deep functional mastery and high-fidelity design refinements.

## Key Features in v2.7.9

### 1. Advanced Milestone System (10 Milestones)
- **Comprehensive Tracking**: Expanded the achievement system to 10 fully functional milestones:
    - **First Step**: Your first fact read.
    - **The Scholar** (10 facts), **The Thinker** (50 facts), and **Master of Mind** (100 facts).
    - **The Curator** (5 saves) and **Librarian** (20 saves).
    - **The Explorer**: Tracks category diversity (5 categories).
    - **Socialite**: Tracks engagement via the share system (3 shares).
    - **Early Bird** & **Night Owl**: Time-sensitive reading milestones (before 8 AM / after 10 PM).
- **Interactive Insights**: Every achievement card now features a detailed dialog with a unique psychology-related quote or insight.
- **Visual Polish**: Removed corner shadows for a clean "flat" design that integrates perfectly with the Home screen.

### 2. Universal Design & Accessibility
- **Theme-Driven Scaling**: Implemented universal text scaling at the theme level. Every element in the app (headings, body, buttons) now scales proportionally with a single setting.
- **Refined Navigation Radii**: Adjusted corner radii for a more professional look: 24dp for the floating nav container and 12dp for the sliding indicator pill.

### 3. Gesture & Motion Mastery
- **Hub Swiping**: Full horizontal swipe navigation between the Home, Explore, Saved, and Settings hubs.
- **Motion Unity**: Standardized the "bubble" entrance animations across the entire app using a shared animation framework.
- **Refined Transitions**: Polished the directional sliding logic to support deep sub-pages and utility screens seamlessly.

### 4. Technical Infrastructure
- **Time-Aware Analytics**: Upgraded the repository to track fact reading timestamps and app-wide sharing activity.
- **Functional Synergy**: Connected all settings (Goals, Accessibility, Haptics) to their real-time functional impacts.
